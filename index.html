SYSTEM // TRANSPARENCY

PRIVACY PROTOCOL

What the application does — and does not — do.

  • Passwords, encryption keys, plaintext, files and QR images are processed in the browser and are not uploaded by the app.
  • Theme, language, favorites and recent module names may be stored locally in this browser only.
  • The URL inspector is a heuristic, not live threat intelligence or malware scanning.
  • JWT inspection decodes data but does not verify signatures.
  • The QR scanner requires HTTPS and browser support for BarcodeDetector.
  • This application cannot inspect the full device, installed apps, system files, Wi-Fi, or device malware. It is not antivirus software.
  • Cyber Lab modules are isolated educational simulations; they do not attack external systems.
